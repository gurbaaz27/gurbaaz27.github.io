# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Friends, collaborators, recruiters, and readers who want to learn about Gurbaaz Singh or read his technical and personal writing.

## Product Purpose

Present Gurbaaz's identity, background, projects, résumé, and long-form posts in a fast, durable personal website. Success means the existing site remains familiar and every published URL continues to work after the framework migration.

## Brand Personality

Personal, thoughtful, and technically curious. The site should feel like a direct conversation with its author rather than a marketing property.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the site into a generic SaaS landing page, an over-designed portfolio template, or an AI-styled editorial redesign. Do not replace the established Beautiful Jekyll identity during maintenance work.

## Design Principles

- Preserve identity before modernizing implementation.
- Let writing and personal imagery remain the focus.
- Keep navigation and interactions obvious and lightweight.
- Prefer durable static output and stable URLs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, alt text, and reduced-motion support while preserving the existing visual character.
